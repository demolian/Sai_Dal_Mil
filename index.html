About the mill

A neighbourhood mill, run the honest way

Tucked in Jamwadi on the edge of Hingoli, Sai Agro Products and Dall Mill takes raw pulses from Marathwada's fields and turns them into the dal that lands on your plate. Cleaning, de-husking, splitting and grading all happen on site, in batches small enough that nothing sits around longer than it should.

Whether you need a couple of kilos for the house or regular sacks for your shop, the process is the same: sort it properly, mill it carefully, pack it clean.

  • Pulses sourced from farmers across the Hingoli–Marathwada belt.
  • Cleaning, milling and grading done on the same premises — nothing outsourced.
  • Open all seven days, 9 AM to 5 PM, for walk-ins and bulk orders alike.
  • Retail packs for households, wholesale quantities for kirana stores and traders.

What we mill

Five dals, milled to order

Every batch is cleaned and split fresh — tell us the dal and the quantity, and we'll let you know what's ready.

Toor Dal

Arhar / Pigeon pea

The everyday dal of Marathwada kitchens — split and polished for a smooth, quick-cooking texture.

Chana Dal

Split Bengal gram

Split from whole chana and cleaned of husk — holds its shape well for curries and snacks.

Moong Dal

Split green gram

Split and de-husked for a light, fast-cooking dal — a household staple across ages.

Masoor Dal

Split red lentil

Cleaned and split to a fine, even grade that cooks down quickly into a soft dal.

Urad Dal

Split black gram

Split and washed, ready for dal makhani, idli-dosa batter, or a simple tempered dal.

How it's made

From sack to store, in five steps

1

Sourcing

Raw pulses bought directly from farmers and traders in the region.

2

Cleaning & sorting

Grain is sieved and sorted to remove stones, dust and broken pieces.

3

Milling

Pulses are de-husked and split into dal on site.

4

Grading

Split dal is graded for size and quality before packing.

5

Packing & dispatch

Weighed and packed to order — for pickup, delivery, or bulk collection.

01

Milled in small batches

Nothing is milled far ahead of when it's needed, so what you get has been sitting for days, not months.

02

Local sourcing

Pulses come from growers around Hingoli, keeping the supply chain short and traceable.

03

Retail or wholesale

A few kilos for the kitchen or full sacks for a shop — both are handled the same careful way.

04

Easy to reach
